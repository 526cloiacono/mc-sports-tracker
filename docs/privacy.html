Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy describes how MC Sports handles information in the Inventory & Profits tracker. For privacy questions, contact MC Sports.

Information you provide

When you register, we collect your first and last name, email address, and authentication information. Supabase Auth handles passwords. Your workspace stores the card details, certification numbers, purchase costs, sales, fees, expenses, shows, locations, dates, times, and notes that you choose to enter. Please do not enter payment-card details, passwords in notes, or sensitive information about other people.

How information is used

We use this information to provide accounts, save and retrieve your records, calculate totals and profit, suggest names from your own saved cards, and support account and security requests. Saved card-name suggestions come from your workspace. They are not a public directory.

Storage and service providers

Supabase provides account authentication and cloud database storage. The configured database is in the US East region. Your browser stores sign-in session information to keep you signed in. Older browser-only records may remain on your device until you clear them. Downloads and backups stay wherever you save them.

The website host receives ordinary web requests, which can include IP address, browser information, and request logs. Google Fonts receives requests when the app loads its fonts. Supabase and these providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. MC Sports does not include advertising trackers or an analytics SDK in this app and does not sell your workspace records.

Who can access records

Workspace records are restricted to the signed-in account by database access rules. Authorized service administrators and providers may access information when needed to operate, secure, or support the service, or comply with applicable law. Records are not displayed as a public collection.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or remove individual records in the tracker and download a JSON backup. To request account deletion, a copy of your information, or a correction you cannot make yourself, follow the account-deletion and privacy-request instructions. Requests are handled manually and require ownership verification. Signing out does not delete your account.

Account and workspace information is retained while your account remains active unless you remove it or request deletion. After a verified deletion request is completed, operational records are removed; residual provider backups and security logs may remain under provider retention schedules or legal requirements. Copies you downloaded and browser records you retained must be deleted separately.

Security and children

We use authenticated access and database ownership controls, but no online service can guarantee complete security. Keep your password private and maintain backups. This service is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has supplied personal information, contact us to request its removal.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes.
