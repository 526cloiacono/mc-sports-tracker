Contact & account requests

Need help with your collection or account? Contact MC Sports through our official Instagram.

Message @mc_sports27 ↗

Opening Instagram does not send a message. Send a private direct message; do not post account details in public comments. If you cannot use Instagram, visit our Dotcard for other available contact options.

Request account deletion

  1. Download a JSON backup from the tracker if you want to keep your records.
  2. Send @mc_sports27 a private message saying “MC Sports tracker — account deletion request.”
  3. We will arrange verification of the registered account before deleting the account and its cloud workspace.

This is a manual request, not immediate deletion. Opening this page or sending a message does not delete any records. Never send your password, recovery link, or sign-in code over social media. We must verify account ownership before acting.

Other privacy requests

Use the same contact to request an export, correction, or help accessing your account. Describe the issue without including your collection’s financial details. Your account email may be needed privately to locate your account.

After deletion

Cloud workspace records and the account are removed when a verified request is completed. Provider backups and security logs may remain under provider retention schedules or legal obligations. Delete local browser records and downloaded copies separately. See the Privacy Policy.
